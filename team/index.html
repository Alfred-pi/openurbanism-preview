03About us

We are a foundation. We are non-profit. We are architects. We are urban planners. We just want to make the urban transition real.

The Open Urbanism Foundation is led by Alain Renk. It develops the Unlimited Cities tool and carries research projects such as Multigination, with a team in Switzerland, Turkey and Finland.

For questions regarding the project, please contact Alain Renk at alain.renk@urbanismeouvert.ch.
