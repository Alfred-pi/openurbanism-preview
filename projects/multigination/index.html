Tool
Unlimited Cities
Coordination
Alain Renk

The tool produces results that create the conditions necessary for the transition.

Multigination starts from a simple point: bring planning to where people are, and let them imagine the future of a place through collages created on site. The research has dramatically accelerated this approach with three advances that are already operational: questionnaires, voting and dashboards.

From individual expression to collective choices; from visions to evidence; from supported experiments to autonomous deployment.

By “tool”, we mean both the process and the digital platform.

AActions in the fieldIn a process of building trust

These actions generate qualitative and quantitative data, which are shared to help create a shared vision.

The team goes with a very visual tool and with mediators: in the street, in front of schools, in companies, to meet the people who rarely go to the town hall. “Have you ten minutes, or five, to give your insight about the future of the place?”

1ImpactArticulating academic research and action in the field

When people analyse their own data, they begin to change the ways they live to follow a project they were part of deciding. Speaking theoretically about active mobility, people do not really understand. When they build, with the tool, a street with playgrounds for children and fewer car places, they do it themselves. Later, when the city presents a project, they become ambassadors of the transition.

Winterthur (CH) pilot in figures

  • 259in situ collages
  • 1,776elements placed in the collages
  • 28%of placed elements tagged soil permeability, the most frequent tag
  • 71%of respondents said their vision, if built, would replace car trips
  • ≈14 tof CO₂ avoided per year among respondents (preliminary)
  • 91%would come on foot, by bike or by public transport
  • 79%would use the place several times a week

Preliminary result based on 169 survey answers with distance, estimated at 125 g CO₂/km, Swiss car fleet average. A potential estimate, not a measured reduction.

2InnovationFrom resistance to change to the evolution of mindsets

01Questionnaires

Questionnaires developed by the researchers go beyond what people imagine, to understand why, and what their visions could change. They deepen the qualitative evidence and make quantitative estimates possible.

02Voting

Individual visions become collective choices. Professionals develop alternatives informed by the participants’ collages, and people vote on them, weaving together bottom-up aspirations and top-down expertise. First conceptualised and tested in Başakşehir (TR).

03Dashboards

Researchers, cities and civic organisations can now deploy the approach independently, using the dashboards.

3DisseminationTeaching free software

Because the tools are open source, others can adapt them, improve them and share those improvements. Unlimited Cities is free software (CC BY-SA and AGPL 3.0).

4Scaling upCreating the conditions for a commons to develop

Open source opens a new business model. The tool is shared for free, and anybody can share it for free, not only the team: a city, or an architect from South Africa or anywhere else, can share it and then ask to be paid for the time spent implementing it.

read the full textclose

This is how the big companies in open software earn their money. It is real business with open source, and Multigination is an open-source approach carried by a non-profit foundation of architects and urban planners.

Within months, four cities outside Multigination deployed the method and are evolving it: Eindhoven (NL), Helmond (NL), Fribourg (CH), Aguascalientes (MX). The dynamic is launched.
