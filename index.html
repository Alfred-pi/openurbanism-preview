00The foundation

Most of the time, people think it is difficult to be in contact with citizens. In fact, it is more difficult to be heard by urban planners and cities. The foundation works to connect bottom‑up and top‑down approaches.

Its tools are open source and shared for free: a digital commons that anyone can adapt, improve and implement. Collective intelligence as infrastructure.

Multigination

Connecting bottom‑up and top‑down approaches: collages made on site, questionnaires, votes and dashboards, so that cities hear what citizens imagine.

02Unlimited Cities, the tool

  1. 01

    Collage on site

    People imagine the future of the place where they stand, with an image and a comment.

  2. 02

    Questionnaires

    Researchers ask why, and what each vision could change in daily life.

  3. 03

    Vote

    Professionals turn the collages into alternatives; participants choose between them.

  4. 04

    Dashboards

    Cities, researchers and civic groups read the data and run the approach on their own.
